What is a Hiiclub Host?

A Hiiclub Host is an individual who registers under a Hiiclub Agency and works on live chat applications. Hosts participate in live streams, voice chats, and interactive sessions on the platform.


As a host, you work under the guidance and support of an agency partner who helps you get set up, understand the platform, and maximize your performance and earnings.

Why Become a Host?

  • No prior experience required
  • Earn based on your platform activity
