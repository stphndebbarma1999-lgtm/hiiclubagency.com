What is the Hiiclub app?

Hiiclub is a video chat and live-streaming app. Hosts receive beans through video calls and gifts, while agencies recruit and support hosts and earn commission based on eligible video earnings.

At hiiclubagency.com, we explain the joining process and the policy information available to us, so you can understand the requirements before applying.

Hiiclub video-call rates and beans

The supplied Hiiclub policy lists the following rates by call duration. It states that 1,000 beans equal ₹1.

Hiiclub video-call rates by call duration (supplied policy)
Call durationBeans per minuteINR equivalent per minute
More than 30 seconds, up to 120 seconds8,700₹8.70
More than 120 seconds, up to 300 seconds9,200₹9.20
More than 300 seconds, up to 600 seconds9,500₹9.50
More than 600 seconds10,000₹10.00

The policy also describes gift earnings and higher-ranked hosts being able to change their call price.

These rupee figures are conversions, not guaranteed income or confirmed take-home pay. Check the rate and conditions shown in your account. The document does not explain billing when a call crosses between duration bands.

Weekly settlement and agency withdrawals

The withdrawal section of the supplied policy describes a Monday-to-Sunday settlement week in IST.

It says host income is transferred to the agency account at 00:00 on Monday, with weekly agency withdrawals by INR bank transfer.

This describes the agency-account credit schedule, not a guaranteed bank-arrival time. The presentation does not specify a withdrawal minimum, processing fee or bank-processing duration; confirm these details in the current dashboard. Hosts should confirm their own payment arrangements separately. This agency-withdrawal section does not establish direct host self-withdrawal.
