Choose Your Route

Hiiclub Agency vs Host: Which Should You Choose?

Both routes start with an application, but the roles are different. Pick the one that matches what you actually want to do.

🏢

Agency: you run a team

  • You recruit adult hosts and help them register
  • You guide your hosts on app rules and answer their day-to-day questions
  • You follow your team’s activity in the agency account the app provides
  • Agency income comes from commission under the app’s current policy, which you should confirm before applying
Read the agency application guide
🎙️

Host: you go live yourself

  • You create a host profile and take part in live chat on the app
  • You must be 18 or over and complete any checks the app asks for
  • You join under an agency, which can help with onboarding
  • Host earnings follow the app’s own rules, which can change
Read the host registration guide

Existing Agencies

Hiiclub Agency Login and OMS Help

If your agency is already registered, you do not need to apply again. Agency accounts are managed in the app’s agency system, which many agencies call the OMS or agency dashboard.

Signing in to your agency account

  • Sign in only through the link or app screen you were given when your agency was approved. Bookmark it once you have it.
  • Check the web address before you type anything. If a page asks you to pay to unlock your account, or someone asks you to share an OTP, stop.
  • Lost your sign-in link or locked out? Message us on WhatsApp with your agency name, or the agency ID shown in your account if you have it. We will point you to the current access route or the right support contact.
  • A new agency application is not a way to recover an existing account. Ask for help first.

This website has no login page. We never ask for your username, password, or OTP, and nothing you type on hiiclubagency.com is sent to the app.

Before You Apply

Hiiclub Agency Commission and Payments: What to Check First

The app sets commission rates, settlement schedules and withdrawal rules, and it can change them. We do not publish figures we cannot verify. Before you apply, confirm these points from the app’s current policy or your agency dashboard, and keep a copy.

  • How commission is calculated, and which host activity counts towards it
  • How often earnings are settled, and when they become available
  • The minimum withdrawal amount and the payment methods offered
  • Any fees, taxes or deductions taken before payout
  • Which identity or bank checks are needed before your first withdrawal
  • Whether any registration fee applies. Treat anyone selling “guaranteed approval” as a red flag

The full checklist is in the agency guide’s commission section.
