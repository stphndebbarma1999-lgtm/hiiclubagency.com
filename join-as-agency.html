Your Role

What a Hiiclub Agency Does

An agency brings hosts to the app and supports them. Before you apply, make sure these responsibilities suit you.

Building your team

  • Find adults who genuinely want to host, and explain the role honestly
  • Help each new host complete registration and any checks the app asks for
  • Keep a simple record of who joined your agency and when

Supporting your hosts

  • Answer questions about app rules and point hosts to the right support
  • Follow team activity in the agency account the app provides
  • Read policy notices from the app and pass changes on to your hosts

You are responsible for how you recruit. Never recruit anyone under 18, never promise income the app does not guarantee, and never ask hosts for their passwords or OTPs.

Eligibility

Before You Apply: Eligibility and What to Prepare

The application form decides the exact fields, and it can change, so we do not publish a fixed list. Have these ready before you start.

Be 18 or over

We only assist adult applicants. The app’s own terms may set further conditions; read them on the form.

A mobile number you control

Use a number you will keep, because the platform may contact you on it. Add WhatsApp if you want our help.

Details that match your ID

Enter your real name and details. If the platform asks for verification, mismatches can delay or block your application.

Payout details, later

Add bank or payment details only when the app’s own dashboard asks for them. Never send them in chat.

How to Apply

How to Apply for a Hiiclub Agency

The application is completed on the app’s agency form. These steps match the button on this page.

1. Open the form

Tap “Open agency application”. The form opens on oms.hiiclub.live in a new tab. Check the address before you type anything.

2. Fill it in yourself

Enter accurate details in every required field. This website does not see or store anything you submit.

3. Save your confirmation

After you submit, screenshot any confirmation, agency name or ID that appears. You will need it if you ask for help later.

4. Ask if anything is unclear

Message us on WhatsApp at any stage. WhatsApp is for help only; it is not a second way to apply.

The application link includes our invite code, which records our agency as the one that referred you. Use it only if you are happy to apply through us.

After Submission

What Happens After You Submit

The platform reviews your application. It may contact you, or ask for extra verification, on the number you applied with. Both the outcome and the timing are the platform’s decision. We cannot guarantee approval or a deadline, and nobody outside the platform can.

If your application is approved, you receive access to your agency account through the app’s agency system. Save that sign-in route and use it every time; you do not need this website to log in.

While you wait, do not submit duplicate applications. If nothing has happened after a while, message us on WhatsApp with the name you applied under and the date, and we will help you check what is missing.

Your Team

Onboarding Hosts to Your Agency

A good start for each host saves you support time later.

Confirm they are adults

Only onboard people aged 18 or over who can pass the app’s own checks.

Share the host guide

Send recruits the host registration guide so they know the requirements before they apply.

Explain earnings honestly

Tell hosts that earnings follow the app’s rules and are not guaranteed. Point them to the policy instead of quoting numbers.

Check they appear in your account

After a host is approved, confirm they show in your agency account. Raise any mismatch with platform support quickly.
