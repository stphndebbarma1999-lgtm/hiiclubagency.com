What to Include in Your Message

Helpful details

  • Your topic: agency application, host registration, agency login, or a payment question
  • If you have applied: the name you applied under and the date
  • If you have an agency: its name, or the agency ID shown in your account
  • A screenshot of any error message, with personal details cropped out

Please don’t send

  • Passwords or OTPs
  • Full identity documents
  • Bank card numbers or banking passwords
Scope

What We Can and Can’t Help With

We can help with

  • Choosing between the agency and host routes
  • The application steps and requirements
  • Working out what an application still needs
  • Finding the right login or support route for an existing agency

Handled by the app

  • Approving or rejecting applications
  • Unlocking or changing accounts
  • Processing earnings and withdrawals

For these, we will point you to the right route.
