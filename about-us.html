About Us

About Hiiclub Agency

We help people find the right route for Hiiclub agency and host registration.

Who Runs This Site

An Independent Registration Support Team

This website is run by a team that works with the Hiiclub app as a referring agency. The agency and host application links on this site carry our invite code, so the platform records our agency as the one that referred you.

We are not the app’s developer, and this is not the app’s official website. The app’s own terms and policies apply to every account, whoever referred it.

We support applicants remotely by WhatsApp. We do not run an office for visitors.

What we do

  • Explain the difference between the agency and host routes
  • Link to the current application pages
  • Answer onboarding questions on WhatsApp
  • Help existing agencies find the right login or support route
  • Share updates through our WhatsApp channel

What we don’t do

  • Approve or reject applications, which only the platform can do
  • Access, unlock or change app accounts, payments or bans
  • Collect applications, passwords or OTPs on this website
  • Guarantee approval, income or response times
How We Help

How Our Support Works

WhatsApp messages

Message +91 9233262700. We reply as soon as we can, but we do not offer 24/7 support or fixed response times.

India, remote

We help applicants across India, entirely online. Support is in English.

Minimal information

We only ask for what your question needs. We never ask for passwords, OTPs or full identity documents.

Accuracy

How We Keep This Information Accurate

App policies change. We do not publish commission rates, withdrawal limits, fees or payment dates unless we can verify them from a current official source. Our guides tell you which points to confirm and where to look.

If you spot something on this website that looks out of date, please tell us so we can check and correct it.
