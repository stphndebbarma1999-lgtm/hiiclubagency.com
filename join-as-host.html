Requirements

Hiiclub Host Requirements

Check these before you apply. Other conditions, such as activity expectations, are set by the app and can change, so we do not quote figures we cannot verify.

18 or over

Hosting is for adults. If you are under 18, do not apply. We will not help you register.

A suitable smartphone

A phone that can install and run the current version of the app, plus a stable internet connection.

Your own, accurate profile

Use your real details and photos of yourself. Profiles using someone else’s identity or photos can be rejected or removed.

Agree to the app’s rules

Read the app’s terms and community guidelines. They decide what is allowed during live chat.

How to Apply

How to Register as a Hiiclub Host

These steps match the “Open host application” button on this page.

1. Open the host application

The form opens on theamigas.com in a new tab. Check the address before you type anything.

2. Complete it yourself

Enter accurate details. This website does not receive or store what you submit.

3. Follow the next instructions

Follow the steps shown after you submit. These may include installing the app and finishing your profile.

4. Wait for review

The platform decides whether to approve you, and when. Nobody can guarantee approval or a timeline.

5. Ask us if you get stuck

Message us on WhatsApp with the step you are on. Help is separate from applying.

The application link includes our agency’s invite code, which associates your host account with our agency. Use it only if you are happy to join through us.

Verification

Profile and Identity Verification

The app may ask you to verify your profile or identity before you can host. These checks happen inside the app, and the app decides the result.

  • Complete verification only on the app’s own screens, following its instructions exactly.
  • Use your own identity, face and photos. Never borrow someone else’s details or documents.
  • Do not try to bypass checks or create several accounts. That can get an account blocked.
  • We never ask you to send identity documents, passwords or OTPs on WhatsApp.
