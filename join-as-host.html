How to Register

Hiiclub Host Registration Steps

Registering as a Hiiclub Host is a simple guided process. Follow these steps to complete your host registration and start your journey on the platform.

💬

Reach Out

Contact us via the WhatsApp icon at the bottom-right corner. Let us know you are interested in registering as a Hiiclub Host.

📋

Provide Information

Share basic personal details with our support team. This information is needed to match you with a suitable agency partner and complete your host registration.

🏢

Agency Assignment

You will be assigned to or connected with a registered Hiiclub Agency. Your agency partner will take responsibility for your onboarding and platform setup.

📱

Platform Setup

Your agency partner guides you through the platform registration and profile setup process. Ensure you follow all guidelines for a smooth approval experience.

✅

Get Approved

After completing your profile and setup, your host registration is reviewed and approved. You will receive confirmation to begin hosting activities on the platform.

🎙️

Start Hosting

Begin participating in live sessions on the platform. Your agency partner and our support team are available to assist you as you get settled into hosting.
