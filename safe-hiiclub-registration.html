How to Check a Hiiclub Registration Link Before You Apply

Registration links are shared widely on social media and in chat groups. A few checks before you type anything protect your details and your account.

By the hiiclubagency.com support team · Published 29 October 2026

1. Read the web address, not just the page

A page can copy any logo or colour scheme, but the address bar shows where you really are. Before you enter details, check the full domain and make sure the page loads over https://.

The application buttons on this website open these external pages:

  • Agency application: a form on oms.hiiclub.live, the Hiiclub agency system.
  • Host application: an invitation page on theamigas.com.

If a link you were sent opens somewhere else, stop and ask the person who sent it to explain. You can also message us to check a link before you use it.

2. Understand what an invite code does

Agency and host links often end with an invite code, for example parent_invite_code=…. The code tells the platform which agency referred you. Our links carry our code. A code is not a password, and nobody should ask you to pay for one.

Choose who refers you on purpose. If someone you already know is your agency, use their link rather than ours.

3. Never share a password or OTP

One-time passwords (OTPs) prove that you are the account owner. Anyone who has your OTP can act as you. Genuine support does not need it.

  • Enter passwords and OTPs only on the app’s own screens.
  • Do not send screenshots that show an OTP, a password, or full card or bank numbers.
  • We will never ask for any of these on WhatsApp.

4. Treat guarantees and upfront fees as warning signs

Only the platform decides whether an application is approved, and earnings depend on the app’s rules. Be cautious if anyone:

  • promises guaranteed approval, a fixed salary, or a set monthly income;
  • asks for a payment to “speed up” or “unlock” an application or account;
  • pressures you to decide immediately, or to move to a different app or payment method.

If a fee really is part of a process, it should appear in the app’s own terms or dashboard, not only in a chat message.

5. Fill in the form yourself and keep a record

Complete applications yourself, with your own accurate details. Don’t let someone else submit them on your behalf with details you can’t see. After you submit, screenshot the confirmation and note the date. That record makes any follow-up much quicker.

6. Already registered? Don’t apply again

A fresh application is not a way to recover an existing agency or host account. Use the sign-in route you were given, or read our agency login help.

7. If something feels wrong

Stop before entering any more details. If you have already shared a password or OTP, change your password in the app straight away and contact the app’s support. To check a link or ask what to do next, message us on WhatsApp at +91 9233262700. That is our only support number.
